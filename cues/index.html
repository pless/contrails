How

Camera position first: the red dot on the map is where the camera is thought to be. If you can tell where it really is (the building or mast it looks from), press set the camera's position and click it. Everything else depends on it.
Landmark: click a point in the picture, then the same place on the map, and say what it is. If you cannot find it on the map, the description alone is still saved. Two landmarks far apart left and right give the heading and the field of view.
Vertical edge: click the top and the bottom of a building edge, mast or pole. Two or more give tilt and roll.
Horizon: click along a sea horizon or far, flat land. Not mountains.
Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Click a cue in the list (or in the picture, with its tool active) to select it; Delete removes it; Shift-click moves the selected landmark. Everything saves by itself, in this browser: when you are done, press download my cues (top left) and send the file to Robert.
